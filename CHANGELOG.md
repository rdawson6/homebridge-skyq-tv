# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-28)

- Added Channel Up and Channel Down buttons to each TV accessory (`channel_buttons`, on by default). They press once, reset themselves, and are ignored while the box is in standby.
- The TV tile updates to the new channel about 2.5 seconds after a button press if it's a favourite.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

- First release. Each Sky Q box is a HomeKit TV with on/standby power, favourite channels as inputs, current-channel tracking and the Control Centre remote.
